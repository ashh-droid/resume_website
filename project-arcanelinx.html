Challenge

Make complex industrial services easy to understand and act on.

The company offers software, automation, optimisation, retrofit, and engineering services. The website needed to organise that range clearly without losing the technical credibility expected by industrial customers.

My contribution

A responsive website with a clear enquiry path.

01

Information architecture

Organised services and company information into a clear, navigable structure.

02

Responsive interface

Designed layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

03

Quotation workflow

Built a guided enquiry flow with field validation and useful error feedback.

04

Search and business content

Added SEO metadata, structured data, downloadable company profiles, and legal pages.

05

Deployment workflow

Used GitHub version control, automated build validation, and Cloudflare deployment.

Experience delivering software for a real business.
